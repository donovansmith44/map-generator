map-only type restriction preserves the original object-only author format.

omitted fields remain distinct from invalid nulls. The kind adapter feeds a
library-deserialized string to the enum's own Deserialize, preserving the original

parsing their messages; user-facing diagnostics render only at the compiler's
outer failure boundary. Reasons and sources remain free authored prose.
Ids remain the existing EntityId throughout the declaration row and interfaces.

EntityKind owns its one Serde spelling/Strum iteration declaration; `kind_of` and
its repeated spelling match disappear. GeometryKind is generated directly from
Feature via Strum EnumDiscriminants, rather than another hand-maintained geometry
vocabulary. WitnessRef and Identity::witness accept that closed type, and every
existing witness caller/test migrates mechanically. Unknown internal geometry
strings are now unrepresentable. Exhaustive matches require a decision for a new
Feature kind; generated laws enumerate every EntityKind, geometry and layer.
